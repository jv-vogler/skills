# writing

Prose editing.

## Skills

### `writing:humanize`

Removes the signs of AI-generated writing so text reads as human-written: inflated symbolism,
promotional language, superficial *-ing* analyses, vague attributions, em dash overuse, rule of
three, AI vocabulary, passive voice, negative parallelisms, filler phrases. Based on Wikipedia's
"Signs of AI writing" guide.
